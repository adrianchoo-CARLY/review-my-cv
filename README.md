# Review My CV, by Career Agility International

Review My CV reads your CV or resume the way a hiring manager or C-suite headhunter does: what they notice in the first 30 seconds, what makes them hesitate, and what gets a candidate shortlisted. It then gives you two Word files:

- **A suggested improved CV**, rewritten only from facts already in your CV. It never invents or inflates employers, titles, dates, numbers, team sizes, budgets or scope, and it tells you what it deliberately did not claim.
- **A visual assessment report** of your original CV: seven rated areas, a health check, a writing verdict and, if you add a job description, a role-fit rating, a level check and a requirement-by-requirement evidence table.

It is built mainly for experienced professionals. It works for early-career CVs too, but check the suggestions carefully.

## How to use it

Say **"Review my CV"**, **"Evaluate my CV"** or **"Improve my CV"** (resume works too), then upload your CV as a Word file, text-based PDF or pasted text. Add a job description if you have a target role.

## What it runs, sends and stores

- It reads the CV and job description you supply in the conversation. Nothing is sent to Career Agility International or any other service.
- The plugin contains instructions and reference files only, with no scripts. Claude creates the two Word files with its own document tools, following the layout guide in `skills/review-my-cv/references/report-layout.md`. The plugin makes no network calls.
- It stores nothing after the conversation, apart from the files it gives you.
- The report ends with one optional mention of CARLY™, Career Agility International's separate AI career agent, with a link. Review My CV never starts CARLY™ or shares your CV with it unless you ask.

## Privacy

Review My CV reads the CV and any job description you share in your own Claude conversation. It sends nothing to Career Agility International or any other service, keeps nothing after the conversation apart from the files it gives you, and only starts CARLY™ if you ask. Career Agility International's privacy policy: https://careeragility.org/privacy-policy

## Important

This AI-assisted CV and assessment are suggestions, not a guarantee of employment; you are responsible for checking all facts and claims before using them. Ratings describe the document, not you or your chances. The method draws on the practice of Adrian Choo and Sze-Yen Chee; the reports are AI-generated, and they do not personally review them.
